Values & Beliefs

"It is for freedom that Christ has set us free. Stand firm, then, and do not let yourselves be burdened again by a yoke of slavery." – Galatians 5:1

Our Core Values

These are the values that as a church we are committed to expressing in all of our activities.

Christ Centered

Everything we do has Christ Jesus as the centre and is aimed at glorifying Him. All that we do is out of love and service for Him.

Relational

A healing community. We will be open and transparent in our actions. A network of friends, accepting and respecting one another. Community, belonging, hospitality and pastoral care are important to us.

Up to Date

Our worship style, our way of doing things and our presentation will be appropriate for the time in which we live. Because the good news about God and forgiveness is always new and always for today.

Pentecostal

The Baptism of the Spirit is important to us. We believe the Gifts of the Spirit and the Fruit of the Spirit are for today. We encourage speaking in tongues.

Family Focused

We will be a church where the whole family is catered for. We are child-friendly. At the same time our goal is to be a multi-generational church, where all ages worship together.

Professional

We will be organized, organic and well presented. We do not want to be just a talk shop. Our services have to meet their objectives. We know why we are here and what we are here to do.

Evangelistic

We want to reach our community with the message about Jesus. We want to see people come to know Him and come into a relationship with Him. This will be a part of everything we do.

Empowering

Our goal is to teach, train and disciple people to be more effective in their lives, their ministries and their giftings.

Releasing

We want to help people find their purpose in life, and the kingdom of God. We want to see everyone active in their faith.

Minimalistic

Church is not a goal in itself, but a means to an end. Therefore we will not seek to create church activities for the sake of it, consuming peoples time. We want them to be free to reach the community around them.

Grace Orientated

Grace is a core value, it is how God responds to us and it is how we will respond to one another. We will forgive and submit to one another out of reverence to Christ.

Generous & Bible Based

God gave to us freely and we want to give as well. Everything we do will be founded on an accurate interpretation of the Scriptures, in all integrity and holiness.
